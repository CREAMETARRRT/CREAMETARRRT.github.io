ABOUT CREAM TART

ABOUT

An independent cinema journal built from first-hand encounters with films.

一本从真实观影、放映与相遇中生长出来的独立电影刊物。

01 / EDITORIAL

What CREAM TART is

CREAM TART is an independent cinema journal focused on independent and underground film, film festivals, filmmaker conversations, screening culture and the everyday life around moving images.

It is a one-person publication. The point of view is deliberately personal: first-hand screenings, festival visits, conversations, programming experiences and things noticed along the way.

奶油蛋挞是什么

CREAM TART / 奶油蛋挞关注独立电影、地下电影、电影节、创作者对谈、放映文化，以及围绕影像发生的日常生活。

这是一本由一个人独立编辑的电影刊物。它保留明确的个人视角：真实参与的放映、电影节、对谈、策展经验，以及一路上被注意到的细节。

02 / SCOPE

What stays here

Screenings — selected records of independent and underground film screenings, guests, Q&As and curated programmes.

Festivals — field notes, reporting and observations from festivals and markets.

Conversations — interviews and exchanges with filmmakers.

Notes — filmgoing, cinemas, travel, objects, industry observations and occasional criticism.

这里留下什么

Screenings — 独立电影与地下电影的放映、嘉宾、映后和策展单元记录。

Festivals — 电影节与电影市场的一手见闻、报道与观察。

Conversations — 与导演及电影创作者的采访与交流。

Notes — 迷影生活、影院、旅行、物件、行业观察与偶尔的电影评论。

03 / LANGUAGE

English + 中文

CREAM TART is being built bilingually. English is the primary interface language; Chinese remains available throughout the site. Older archival pieces may first appear in their original Chinese and receive English versions gradually.

英文 + 中文

奶油蛋挞从一开始就按双语媒体搭建。界面以英文为主，同时保留中文。早期迁移的档案文章可能先以原始中文呈现，再逐步补充英文版本。
